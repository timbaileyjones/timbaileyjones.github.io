## McKinsey Music Festival 2022 — Kitzbühel, Austria

One of the most fantastic corporate perks I've ever had was attending the classical music festival hosted by [McKinsey & Company](https://mckinsey.com), one of the world's premier consulting firms. I had the distinct pleasure of being one of their Cloud Engineers for about 3 years.

In 2022 that meant a trip to Kitzbühel, Austria and [McKinsey's Grand Hotel](https://www.grandhotel-kitz.com/), normally used for employee and client retreats. My wife and I built a larger European vacation around the event — 12 days total, starting in Munich and ending in Salzburg. In the middle of this dream trip, we spent three days rehearsing an elaborate professional arrangement of *An American in Paris* and **Mahler's 2nd Symphony in C minor (*Resurrection*)**.

Somehow I had played band and orchestra music for over 40 years without discovering Mahler. I've been catching up ever since.

The video above is our performance at [Hotel Rasmushof, also in Kitzbühel](https://www.rasmushof.at/en/). I am the bassoon player on the right, in the bright red shirt.
